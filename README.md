# About the Organization

Shivjayanti Utsav Samiti is a non-profit social organization dedicated to serving society through various welfare initiatives, cultural programs, awareness campaigns, helping drives, blood donation camps, educational activities, environmental initiatives, and community development.

A unique identity of this organization is that every active member belongs to the **Goods & Services Tax (GST) Department, Government of Maharashtra**. While serving the nation professionally, the members voluntarily contribute their time and effort towards social welfare.

This website aims to digitally represent the organization's mission, achievements, activities, and legacy while reflecting the inspiring values of **Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj**.

# Design Language

## Color Palette

Primary

- Bhagwa (Saffron)
- Deep Maroon
- Royal Gold

Secondary

- Stone Grey
- Dark Brown
- Ivory
- White

Accent

- Copper

Avoid overly bright modern gradients.

The theme should look royal, timeless and premium.

# Visual Identity

The website should evoke emotions similar to:

- entering a fort
- hearing the sound of war drums
- seeing the Bhagwa flag
- experiencing the glory of Swarajya

Use

- Traditional patterns
